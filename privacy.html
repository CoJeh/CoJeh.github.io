Privacy Notice
隐私说明

This page explains the limited analytics used across Corrine's Portfolio, Dashboard, Playbook and SpotOn pages.

本页说明 Corrine 作品集、Dashboard、Playbook 与 SpotOn 页面使用的有限访问统计。

Effective 26 July 2026 | 2026年7月26日生效

What this site measures

Microsoft Clarity helps measure page visits, clicks, scrolling, session duration, device type, approximate region and referral source. This information is used to improve the portfolio experience.

本站统计什么

本站使用 Microsoft Clarity 了解页面访问、点击、滚动、停留时间、设备类型、大致地区和访问来源。这些信息仅用于改进作品集体验。

Your choice

Analytics cookies are used only after you select Accept. If you decline, Clarity may provide limited cookieless measurement. Your choice is stored in your browser so this notice does not appear again on that browser.

你的选择

只有在你点击“同意”后，本站才会使用分析 Cookie。如果你拒绝，Clarity 可能只提供有限的无 Cookie 统计。你的选择会保存在当前浏览器中，因此该浏览器不会再次显示此提示。

What is protected

  • Feedback form content and avatar areas are masked from Clarity.
  • Names, email addresses and direct identifiers are not intentionally sent to Clarity.
  • Campaign links use general labels such as LinkedIn, CV or recruiter email. They never include a recipient's name.

受到保护的内容

  • 留言表单内容和头像区域会对 Clarity 进行遮罩。
  • 本站不会有意向 Clarity 发送姓名、邮箱或其他直接身份信息。
  • 渠道链接只使用 LinkedIn、简历、招聘邮件等通用标签，不加入收件人姓名。

Service provider

Microsoft processes Clarity data according to its Privacy Statement. Standard session recordings are currently retained for 30 days, while click and heatmap data may be retained for up to 9 months.

服务提供方

Microsoft 根据其隐私声明处理 Clarity 数据。目前普通会话回放数据保留 30 天，点击和热图数据最长可能保留 9 个月。
